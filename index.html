About Us

InAmigos Foundation is dedicated to bringing smiles to the faces of those who need it the most. We believe in the power of community, empathy, and sustainable development. Together, we are creating a world where every individual has access to basic necessities, education, and a healthy environment.

Our Mission

To empower underprivileged communities through sustainable initiatives in education, healthcare, and environmental conservation, ensuring a dignified life for all.

Our Vision

A world free of poverty and inequality, where every individual has the opportunity to thrive in a safe, healthy, and supportive environment.

Our Initiatives

SEVA

Food distribution drives to ensure no one sleeps hungry. We provide nutritious meals to the homeless and daily wage workers.

UDAAN

Education support for underprivileged children. We provide books, stationery, and free tutoring to build a better future.

JEEV

Environmental conservation through massive tree plantation drives and zero-waste awareness campaigns.

Women Empowerment

Skill development and micro-finance initiatives to help women become financially independent and confident.

Health Awareness

Free medical camps, blood donation drives, and hygiene workshops in rural and slum areas.

Community Development

Building shelters, clean water facilities, and organizing community building events to foster togetherness.
